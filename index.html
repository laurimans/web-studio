White Noise

Maya might be a bit stressed, but strange things are starting to happen in her apartment, and the line between reality and illusion is beginning to blur.

White Noise is an introspective narrative adventure about the fragility of routine and the mental noise we use to silence what we are afraid to accept.

  • Explore a shifting apartment that transforms over time
  • Use everyday objects to tune into hidden frequencies
  • Uncover fragments of the truth hidden in the mental noise

An indie game studio based in Madrid

Zarza means bramble. A bramble grows, entangles itself and traps you almost without you realising it. That’s what we aim to achieve with our video games.

We’re a small independent studio in Madrid, founded by two friends, Laura Blázquez and Laura Manso (yes, you can call us "the Lauras"). We make video games that explore everyday life and delve into what makes us human . We want you to feel, whilst playing, that there are two people behind the screen. That’s why every hand-drawn scene, every line of dialogue and every note in our soundtrack is placed there with the utmost care.
